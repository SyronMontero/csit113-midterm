How to Order

Home | Menu | Order | Contact

Ordering Process

  1. Check today's menu and choose your food.
  2. Fill out the pre-order form on the main page.
  3. Enter your full name and contact number.
  4. Select your food item and quantity.
  5. Choose your preferred pickup date.
  6. Click the "Place Pre-Order" button.

Pickup Process

  1. Visit Rene's Turo-Turo on your selected pickup date.
  2. Tell the staff the name used for your order.
  3. Confirm your order and pay at the counter.
  4. Receive your freshly prepared food.

Important Reminder

Please make sure your contact number and order details are correct before submitting the form.
